# Getting Started

## Introduction

### Welcome to Wellows

Your inbound marketing assistant for creating better results with less effort.

## Create an Account and Get Started

Getting started with **Wellows** is simple. Follow these steps to set up your account and access the platform:

### 1. Sign Up
- Visit wellows.com and click on **Create a free account** or **Sign Up**.

### 2. Create an Account
- You can sign up using your **Google account** for a seamless experience.
  **OR**
- Fill in the form with:
  - First Name
  - Last Name
  - Email
  - Password
  - Confirm Password
- Accept the **Terms & Conditions**
- Click **Sign Up** to create your account.

![Create Account Screen](https://wellows.com/wp-content/uploads/wellowsdocs/image45.png)

---

### 3. Sign In

Once registered, log in using:

- **Continue with Google**
  **OR**
- Your registered email and password
- Optionally, check **Trust this device for 30 days** to stay signed in

If you forget your password, click **"I forgot my password"** to easily reset it.

![Sign In Screen](https://wellows.com/wp-content/uploads/wellowsdocs/image60.png)

## SEO Hub Dashboard Screen

After connecting your account, you'll see this dashboard. It's the main SEO Hub in Wellows, showing all available AI agents. From here, you can access live agents like KIVA and OPTA, or get notified when upcoming agents like Content Update, Content Pruning, and CRO go live.

![Sidebar Navigation](https://wellows.com/wp-content/uploads/wellowsdocs/image69.png)

## Sidebar Navigation (Dashboard Overview)

When you sign in to Wellows, the sidebar on the left gives you quick access to all major sections of the platform.

### What You'll Find:
- **Home** – Return to your main dashboard view
- **Workspace** – Start, access, or manage your SEO projects
- **Reports** – View insights, performance data, and keyword reports
- **Brand Guidelines** – Set and manage your brand's content tone and style
- **Billing** – Access your subscription plan, invoices, and usage credits
- **Speak Your Mind!** – Share feedback or suggest improvements directly to the team
- **Join us on Slack** – Connect with the Wellows team or community via Slack

Also visible:
- **GSC Status** – Displays whether Google Search Console is connected
- **Credit Usage** – Shows your remaining credits out of 10
- **Upgrade Button** – Click to explore premium plans and features

## Create Workspace

The **Create Workspace** pop-up allows you to set up a new project space for managing specific campaigns, clients, or content workflows.

- **Workspace Name** – Enter a unique name to identify your project or client
- **Description** – Add a short summary or notes about the purpose of this workspace

Once filled, click **Create** to generate and organize all related SEO activities under this workspace.

## Reports Section

The **Reports** dropdown gives you access to different types of SEO and performance insights across the Wellows platform.

![KIVA Report](https://wellows.com/wp-content/uploads/wellowsdocs/image58.png)

- **KIVA Report** – View a history of all keyword analyses, content briefs, and insights generated by KIVA, organized by date, workspace, and seed keyword.

![ChatRadar Report](https://wellows.com/wp-content/uploads/wellowsdocs/image37.png)

- **ChatRadar Report** – Track the status and history of keyword monitoring across LLMs, including suggested keywords, regions, and tracking activity.

![OPTA Report](https://wellows.com/wp-content/uploads/wellowsdocs/image23.png)

- **OPTA Report** – View real-time audits of live URLs with completion status, total URLs analyzed, and detailed optimization insights.

Use this section to monitor progress, extract learnings, and share data with your team or clients.

## Brand Guidelines

![Brand Guidelines](https://wellows.com/wp-content/uploads/wellowsdocs/image22.png)

The **Brand Guidelines** section lets you define your brand's tone, voice, and target audience, ensuring that all generated content aligns with your business identity.

### Key Features:
- Add guidelines via URL or upload a file
- Tailor tone, writing style, and brand personality to match your audience
- Ensure consistent messaging across every piece of content
- Help KIVA generate content that reflects your unique brand voice

Use this section to guide how your brand communicates—so everything from headlines to long-form content feels on-brand and customer-ready.

The **Speak Your Mind** section is where users can share feedback about their experience with KIVA, helping the team improve features, usability, and performance.

- Click **Generate Your Feedback** to submit your responses

Your input directly shapes future updates, making KIVA better for everyone.
